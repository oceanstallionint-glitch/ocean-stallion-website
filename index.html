About the Company

A new merchant-exporter built around the farm.

OCEAN STALLION INTERNATIONAL is an India-based agricultural commodity merchant-export business being established to connect Indian agricultural sourcing with international buyers.

Our approach is straightforward: we aim to source commodities like maize, wheat, pearl millet, and groundnut directly from farmers and trusted suppliers. Before confirming any supply, we focus on clearly understanding the buyer's specific requirements.

We coordinate product specifications, grading, packaging, and quality checks as applicable. Where direct farmer sourcing is not practical, we buy from trusted local traders (vapari) as a secondary channel. We believe in communicating clearly about availability, quantity, shipment requirements, and documentation, ensuring transparent commercial terms.

Our Commodities

Five core commodities. One sourcing discipline.

Yellow Maize

Sourced mainly from our farmer network. Moisture, foreign matter and grading agreed per buyer specification.

White Maize

White maize procured mainly through our farmer network, with moisture and grading agreed against buyer spec.

Wheat

Milling and feed grade wheat, with quality parameters agreed with the buyer and verified before dispatch.

Pearl Millet (Bajra)

Traditional Indian coarse grain, sourced from farmers and cleaned to buyer specification.

Groundnut

Bold and Java varieties, subject to availability, with oil content discussed against the buyer's requirement.

Other Grains & Pulses
